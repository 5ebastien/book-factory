The howl came from everywhere at once. It did not arrive from one direction. It vibrated in the air under the Tree. The leaves shivered violently with the sound. Grey light flickered against the bark. Muninn lowered his head. He spoke in a small, thin voice. He remembered the last time he heard that howl. It was over a frozen field. The frost had been white and sharp. It was eleven hundred years ago. It was the morning he was lost.

Elsie held the stone tight. It went warm in her hand. It had never done that before. The heat spread to her fingers. It was not a friendly warmth. It was a living, heavy pressure. Sköll had her scent. The howl faded into the grey dark. The leaves stopped moving. Silence rushed back in.

Then a second black shape dropped from the branches. It landed on a low limb with a thud. This raven was quick and restless. He was Huginn. Thought. He was Muninn’s brother. He looked at Elsie with bright, black eyes. He did not offer memories. He did not share the past. He tilted his head and asked a single question.

Did she mean to wait for it?
