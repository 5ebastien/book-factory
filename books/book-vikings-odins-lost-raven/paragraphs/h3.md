The horns blew at first light.
They came over the broken part of the old wall.
The noise was everywhere at once.
Shouting mixed with the clang of iron on wood.
Running feet thudded against the muddy ground.
Smoke rose from a thatch roof that had caught fire.
Elsie stood in the lane between the wattle houses.
The air was thick with grey and black.
It smelled of burning straw and wet earth.
Ketil stood very still beside her.
His face was pale beneath the dawn.
He did not look at the smoke.
He did not look at the running feet.
He looked only at Elsie.
"I am going to hide," he said.
His voice was small and thin.
He pulled at her sleeve.
"Come with me," he asked.
He did not wait for an answer.
He turned and stepped toward the dark gap.
It was between two leaning walls.
The dust swirled around their boots.
Muninn flew up off Elsie’s shoulder.
The raven cut straight into the smoke.
It flew toward the loudest part of the noise.
A memory flickered in Elsie’s mind.
A road ran through a battle.
Women and children ran down the lanes.
They hurried toward the river’s edge.
Elsie’s boots sank into the cold mud.
Ketil was already halfway into the gap.
He looked back once.
His hand was outstretched.
The smoke drifted over Elsie’s head.
It was cold and damp.
She felt the weight of the moment.
The raven was gone.
The lane was empty except for the dust.
Elsie stood in the grey morning.
The mud held her feet fast.
