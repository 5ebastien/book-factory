The little timber house sat on the deck of the ship.
It had been placed there on a bed of sorts.
A feather quilt covered the space.
Her things lay around her in the dark.
The daughter walked in last.
She was a tall woman.
A ring of keys hung at her belt.
She stepped over the threshold.
She stood inside for a moment.
Then she walked back out.
Her face was dry.
There were no tears in her eyes.
She looked calm and in charge.
Every person on the hill stood very still.
Nobody moved their feet.
The wind did not touch the grass.
Elsie watched through the stone.
The sunstone felt warm against her chest.
She looked at the ship in the trench.
It was not in the ground for a second.
The timber disappeared.
Grey water rose up around the hull.
The sail filled with wind.
It pulled the ship forward.
They were sailing on the deep.
Ahead, a shape rose from the mist.
It looked like a great tree.
The water was still and calm.
Then the scene broke.
The ship was back in the earth.
Muninn opened his beak.
The sound was like dry leaves.
He spoke a memory aloud.
"This was a road," he said.
His voice echoed off the soil.
The crowd remained frozen in place.
The daughter held her keys tight.
Elsie looked at the bird.
The raven looked back at her.
The air was cold and still.
