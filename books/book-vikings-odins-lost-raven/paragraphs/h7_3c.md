The gate stood alone on the hillside, very old and locked, with no wall on either side. Muninn remembered the name, though Elsie did not. She walked around it because there was no other way, and she felt silly doing so. The lock was dark and heavy, and the iron was cold under the air. On the far side, the hall was there. It had not been a moment before. The roof was made of shields, and the rafters were spears.

The smell of old wood and dust filled the air. Elsie looked closer at the structure while the silence grew heavy. There were five hundred and forty doors in the long, low building. The brave dead fought inside them all day. They feasted inside them all night. The sounds did not come out. The doors were closed. A single raven sat on the gate post, watching her. Its eyes were black and deep. The light from the sky seemed to stop at the edge of the hill.

Elsie reached out to touch the nearest door. The wood was warm.
