Elsie stepped out of the grey light onto something wide and hard. At first, she thought it was a road made of ice, but the surface was rough and twisted. It went down into the mist, curling in slow loops until it disappeared below. The cold here was different, a heavy weight that pressed against her chest. Somewhere far beneath her feet, water roared out of the stone with a steady, wet sound. Under that roar, there was another noise, slow and endless, like a giant chewing on bone. Muninn sat on her shoulder and tilted his head toward the dark. He did not speak in words, but a memory of the tree's name settled into her mind. Yggdrasil. The word felt like a stone in her stomach, old and heavy. She looked up, but the trunk of the tree was so high that it vanished into the mist. The grey light did not move or change; it just hung there, still and dead. Elsie took a step forward, and the root creaked beneath her trainer. The mist around her knees swirled, thick and cold, hiding what lay down in the dark.
