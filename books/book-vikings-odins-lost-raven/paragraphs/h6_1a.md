Elsie stepped out of the grey onto solid ground. Her trainers gripped the surface, which at first looked like a road. It was wide and flat and stretched straight ahead. Then she saw the texture. It was not tarmac. It was bark. A single root, thick as a motorway, went down into the mist. The cold hit her face hard. It was worse than the sea. It was worse than the freezer at home. It was a cold that had teeth. She shivered, but the shiver did not help. The air was thick with frost and felt heavy. Somewhere below her, water roared out of the earth. It sounded like a storm trapped underground. Under the roar, there was another sound. It was slow and wet and endless. Like something very big chewing. She looked up, but there was nothing above. Only more grey. The light did not change. It never would. Muninn landed on the edge of the root next to her foot. He did not look at her. He looked at the mist. He tilted his head to one side. His feathers were black and stiff with ice. "Yggdrasil," he said. His voice was soft and dry. He said the name like he was remembering an old friend. He said it without any words in between. He just said the name, and held it. The root dipped slightly under her weight. The mist below swirled and rose. The chewing sound got louder for a moment. Elsie tightened her grip on her jacket sleeve. She stared down into the white void. The water hissed against the bark. The root groaned under the weight of the tree above. She did not move. The mist touched her ankles. It was freezing.
