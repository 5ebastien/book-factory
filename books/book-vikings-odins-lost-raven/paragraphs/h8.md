The hall smelled of warm wool and old wood. Outside, the snow fell in thick, slow sheets. Elsie stood still while the noise of the feast rose around her. It was a loud, deep sound, like a great beast breathing in its sleep. Muninn shifted on her shoulder. The raven looked at the rows of shields above.

The roof was made of golden shields. The rafters were long, dark spears. Over the west door, a wolf and an eagle hung in carved stone. They looked ready to fly at any moment. Elsie watched them for a long time. The light in the hall was soft and amber.

Muninn opened its beak. The voice was clear in her mind. It was not a question. It was a memory, sharp and bright. It said that this place was home. The sound faded quickly. The hall continued to hum around them.

A footstep creaked on the stone floor. It stopped nearby, but the owner did not speak. Elsie looked down at her trainers. The stone floor was cold through the thin soles. She felt the weight of the raven on her shoulder. It was still and quiet now.

The feast went on without pause. The smell of food grew stronger. Elsie breathed in the air. It tasted of smoke and iron. She waited for the next step. The silence between the sounds was heavy. The wolf on the door seemed to lean forward slightly. It did not move, but it looked closer.
