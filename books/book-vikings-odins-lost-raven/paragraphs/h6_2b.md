The mist down here was thick and cold, and it smelled of wet stone and old earth. Elsie stood on a root that was wider than the museum benches she knew. Below her, the chewing got louder, a slow and heavy sound that shook the ground under her feet. She looked down into the grey nothing and saw a long dark shape moving in the deep. It was a dragon, and it was gnawing at the base of the World Tree without ever stopping. Muninn ruffled his feathers and spoke without looking at her, his voice flat and dull. The tree always grew back as fast as it was eaten, he said, which was the only reason anything was still standing. Elsie watched the dark shape pull back and bite again, the bark snapping like dry wood. It did not look up at them, and it did not stop. The sound of the chewing filled the quiet air, steady and endless, while the mist swirled around her ankles. She felt the vibration in her bones, a low hum that matched the rhythm of the teeth. The root beneath her feet seemed to shiver, though the tree above remained still and tall.
