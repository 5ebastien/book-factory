Elsie stood in the trampled dirt of the yard, her trainers sinking into the cold mud. Above her, a goat sat on the roof ridge, staring down with flat, unblinking eyes. The heavy doors of the hall stood open, spilling out a roar of laughter. Then the courtyard filled with men. They walked in from the west, in groups of three and four. Their faces were bright, though they looked tired. Some limped. Some held their heads tilted to one side. But the cuts on their arms were closing. She watched a deep wound on a man’s shoulder knit itself shut. The skin smoothed over. The red line vanished. He shrugged and rubbed the spot, laughing at something behind him. Tomorrow they would do it all again. Every day, they fought. Every night, they feasted. It would go on until the world ended. The air smelled of smoke and wet wool. A huge shadow stopped in front of her. He was red-bearded and very wide. His front teeth had grooves filed into them. He looked down at Elsie, then at the black bird on her shoulder. Muninn ruffled his feathers but said nothing. The warrior leaned in closer. He smelled of stale sweat and honey. "Whose girl are you?" he asked. His voice was loud, like a creaking door. "And why is that raven riding on your shoulder?" The yard went quiet around them. A hundred eyes watched. Elsie waited for an answer that was not hers to give. The warrior smiled, his filed teeth gleaming in the fading light.
