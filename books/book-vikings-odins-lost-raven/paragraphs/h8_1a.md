The yard before the hall smelled of wet earth and distant smoke, while the evening light fell flat and grey across the snow. Elsie stood in the trampled mud, her trainers sinking slightly into the cold ground, and felt the weight of the raven on her shoulder. Muninn watched the scene with eyes like black stones, saying nothing at all. A goat stood on the roof above, chewing slowly, and seemed to ignore the chaos below completely.

Then the fighting stopped, and the warriors came in from the field. They were laughing and limping and slapping each other on the back, though their clothes were torn and their skin was raw. Elsie watched closely, and every cut on their bodies began to close and fade, healing while she watched. The pain drained out of their faces, replaced by easy smiles, and the air around them hummed with a strange, warm energy.

Tomorrow they would do it all again, and every day after, until the end of the world. The rhythm of it was steady, like a heartbeat that never skipped, and it made Elsie’s chest feel tight. She did not understand why they came back, or how the wounds could simply vanish so quickly. It was not like any story her grandad had ever told her, or any history lesson at school.

A huge red-bearded warrior stopped in front of her, blocking the path to the hall. Grooves were filed across his front teeth, and his beard was thick and bright as fire. He looked down at her, curious and friendly, and tilted his head to one side.

"Whose girl are you?" he asked, his voice deep and rough. "And why is a raven riding on you?"
