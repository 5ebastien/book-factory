The grey light under the Tree grew dim, then dimmer still. It felt like a cloud moving across a sun Elsie could not see. Through the stone, the bright point began to jerk. It moved with the erratic speed of a rabbit running. Behind it, very far off, something huge ran on long legs. Sköll was closing in. Muninn slipped inside Elsie’s coat without being asked. His feathers were cold against her throat. A second black raven dropped from the branches. It was Huginn, Thought, Muninn's brother, quick and restless and nothing like him. He landed on a low bough and vibrated with energy.

"Are you going to stand there all day?" Huginn asked.

Elsie clutched the stone tighter. It hummed against her palm, cold and heavy. In the vision, the wolf turned its head. It sniffed the air. It was hunting the stone. It was hunting her.

"There was an island in a lake below the Tree," Muninn said from inside her coat. His voice was thick with memory. "Something was tied up on it. It was still there, the last time."

"Or would you rather be caught?" Huginn asked, ruffling his feathers. "Is the open ground not faster? Why wait for the wolf to choose?"

Elsie looked at the twisting roots, then at the open ground. Her heart hammered against her ribs. She did not know which way to go. The light flickered again, a sharp pulse of warning. Sköll’s silhouette in the stone grew larger. The air felt thin and tight. She stared at the stone, trapped between the past and the present.
