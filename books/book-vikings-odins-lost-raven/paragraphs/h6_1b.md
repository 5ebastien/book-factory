Elsie stepped onto the branch. It was as wide as a road. The bark beneath her trainers felt like a cliff. The trunk went up out of sight. It also went down out of sight. The light was grey. It was neither day nor night. It was a dusk that never finished.

Dew dripped from the leaves above. It fell thick as honey. One drop struck her shoulder. It was cold. She wiped it away.

Muninn ruffled his feathers. He did not look at her. He looked at the trunk.

"Yggdrasil," he said.

His voice was soft. It sounded like remembering an old friend. He said the name simply. He did not explain it. He did not point.

Elsie looked around. Everything there was on the tree. Or under it. There was no other place. The ground was far below. It was lost in the grey.

Far down, something chewed. It moved slowly. The sound was a dull crunch. It echoed upward. It was not a bird. It was not a wind.

Far above, something enormous shifted. Wings folded and unfolded. The movement was silent. It was huge. It blocked the grey light for a second. Then the light returned.

Elsie held on to the bark. Her fingers sank into the rough texture. It was wet with the honey-dew. The branch did not sway. The tree did not move. The chewing sound continued. The wings shifted again. Elsie looked at Muninn. He was staring at the trunk. His eyes were dark and still. He waited. Elsie waited. The dew dripped.
